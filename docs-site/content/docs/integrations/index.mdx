The goal: say *"Hey Google, turn on the AC"* or *"set the fan to speed 3"* and
have it reach the [IR blaster](/docs/hardware/ir-blaster) — **without paying for
Nabu Casa Cloud**.

## How the pieces connect

```text
Google Assistant ──▶ Home Assistant (home.pawan67.dev) ──▶ MQTT ──▶ HELM ──▶ ESP32 IR blaster
     (voice)          google_assistant: integration        discovery      applyClimate / fireIrButton
```

HELM already publishes the AC as a `climate` entity and the fan as a `fan` entity
to Home Assistant over MQTT discovery. So there are only two things to wire up:

<Cards>
  <Card title="Google Home" href="/docs/integrations/google-home" description="Link Home Assistant to Google via the free Cloud-to-cloud integration." />
  <Card title="Home Assistant" href="/docs/integrations/home-assistant" description="The google_assistant: config block and which entities to expose." />

## Prerequisites

- Home Assistant reachable over **public HTTPS** (this deploy uses
  `https://home.pawan67.dev/`).
- A Google account (the one on your phone).
- The AC + fan visible in HA under **Settings → Devices → HELM Bar Node**.
- The [IR blaster](/docs/hardware/ir-blaster) wired, if you want it to actually
  do something physical.

<Callout type="info" title="Free vs. Nabu Casa">
Nabu Casa Cloud does this in one click for ~$6.50/mo. The
[Google Home guide](/docs/integrations/google-home) does the same thing for free,
at the cost of ~30–45 min of Google Cloud console setup — a one-time thing.
</Callout>

## Want something working today?

While you set up Google, HELM's own **Shortcuts** give you instant voice control
with no accounts and no subscription — see
[Voice shortcuts](/docs/integrations/voice-shortcuts).
